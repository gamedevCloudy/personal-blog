Games have played a very integral part in my life.

Ever since I was little, I have been an avid gamer - from what we used to call a "video game" (an NES clone console) to what I have today (a tablet).

Here's a list of my favourite games, in no particular order.

## What Kind of Gamer Am I?

Based on this list, here's what Claude thinks of the kind of gamer I was, and the kind I am now:

- Kid: Pick-up-and-play, and mostly about shooting things - Contra, Duck Hunt, IGI, causing chaos in Vice City. Games were something you shared, with a cousin or with your dad.
- Teenage: The competitive grinder. Fighting games, zombie shooters and then battle royales, played until you were genuinely good (top 1% in COD-M). Racing was always on the side.
- Early Adulthood: Still coming back to PUBG, but now also playing to unwind (Minecraft, Alto's Odyssey, Cats & Soup, Lichess puzzles) and to appreciate how games are made - Hollow Knight came through GMTK videos, not hype.

## Mobile Gamers Are Gamers

During discussions, people often try to marginalize "mobile gamers" as not really gamers. But a phone was the most accessible hardware I have ever had. Across the 4-5 phones I've owned, I have put 1000s of hours into gaming.

A game is a game; the hardware is only a medium. It's the game developer's job to give you an engaging and delightful experience.

Thanks to all the devs of the games who made my gaming memorable <3!
