A little list on health and sporty adventures.

During childhood, I wasn't very sporty, but I have always been competitive, you know, not the best player - but the runner-up.

I would participate in almost all the sports the school let me.
I have played:

- badminton doubles
- football - defence (and goalkeeping during our sports periods)
- karate

for the school team.

During annual sports days, I have done:

- karate performances
- running 100m
- relay (cuz our house didn't have the best players)
- long jump
- discus
- kabaddi

We also had an adventure sports circuit, which I was really good at and competitive in with my peers.

I have won medals in karate btw - State Level Gold.

And then I left the sport in pursuit of academic success, as my class 8 mathematics had regressed by then.
Pretty standard for Asian countries: we're expected to perform well in school, and the rest comes afterwards.

From there, I only did a little bit of "home workouts".

Skipping to the future:

1. Gym

For the better part of college, I struggled with my immunity going down and falling sick. I also had 2 wisdom teeth surgically removed within a year, plus another minor surgery.

The fourth year of my college was a redemption arc - to address all the issues.

Fourth year of engg in India is mostly to prepare for placements or work on internships (should have done some leetcode).
Every day I would wake up to 2-4 rejection emails. At some point, I wouldn't even recall that I had applied there.

After a point, this would have gotten very depressing, so I knew I had to keep my mind busy and fresh.

In the mornings, I would hit the gym, study a little, and then work on my internship. Mostly throughout the day at a relaxed pace - and even on weekends. (shouldn't have; have a personal life)

I was very weak during the first week and it only improved from there. My body quickly picked up pace - improving strength and physique.

Gym stopped after I moved back home from college.

2. Swimming

I used to try to go swimming during college, and it basically depended on the public pool being in working condition and us getting reservations.

I knew I wanted to try it, so when I came back home, during WFH, I decided to learn it.

Started this right after my bday last year.

It made me very healthy and mentally resilient - like taking cold showers and then throwing your body in the water and watching it fight as you do laps.

At peak time, I was swimming in 10°C waters lol.
I was in a very nice, happy period, definitely "peak".

Misadventures: I think I dislocated my shoulder once and now it makes little noises. It's mainly due to "general laxity" - meaning joint restrainers are a little stretchy allowing more movement than required - which I found out almost a year later.

I went for Oct-Nov; after that, the pool shut down for winter.

---

In general, I would like to resume some activity - I couldn't really pick up gym because the full-time in-office job was very taxing mentally and physically.

Those were my sporty adventures and misadventures. Keep up some sport!
