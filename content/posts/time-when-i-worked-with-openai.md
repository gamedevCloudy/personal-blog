## How I Wrote 2 Ads for ChatGPT

This was round Feb 2026. I had freshly joined our new office in Bengaluru after working remotely for 6 months.

### Background

I worked at a startup, who's founder was also a content creator.

Part of our business was - helping people adopt AI in their workflows.
We had been working towards how it can be adopted towards a daily user.

Speaking about the content creator, they make AI use case videos - trying out a varying range of things.

So naturally, we had become closer with OpenAI.

(I had previously worked on a project for the only OpenAI employee in India once - before they established their headquarters in New Delhi)

### The Problem

So, when OpenAI was looking for expansion in the Indian market - they came to us for ideas.

You might think oh come-on they had enough users already.
But you need to use AI for things other than making Ghibli Art with it.

That was our problem statement. People were using AI but not to solve their daily problems.

Specifically, OpenAI / ChatGPT team found it tough to adapt the features of AI into daily use cases - that Actual Indians (pun intended) could try.

They wanted to do a creator led campaigns - but creators themselves found it difficult to put AI in their daily life.

### How I Fit In

I had been using AI since late 2022 - when GPT first launched.

First to play, then to write assignments and prepare for exams and later (when it got better) - to help me write code.

Initially, my approach had been of a "Tech First" user - I am part of a niche audience who devour tech videos (LTT, Jeff Geerling, ThePrimeagen).

That was OpenAI's problem too.
AI fit into tech people's hand like the perfect pair of gloves.

But to the rest - it sounded like a weird device that they didn't know how to put on.

If I had come with this approach, I would have failed drastically.

But, I am part creative as well. Given my history of making videos, writing scripts and (at least) trying to make content - I enjoyed one thing - telling stories.

### So Back to Pen and Paper (Notion Actually)

Our task was clear. First 10 ideas - decide we get do to more of this. And the possibility of a partnership with OpenAI.

Everyone on the team was to write 5–10 ideas.

I just worked on 3 for my first round.
After internal feedback from our leader - many were rejected.
2 of my ideas were accepted from this round.

Thus, I was given the responsibility of handling this deliverable.
Now that we had the crude idea of what it looked like, next I wanted to connect this to a feeling - a story. Since these were to become prompts for content creators.

For each idea, I broke it down into these:

- The feeling I wanted to invoke
- The setup: what it could feel like
- The problem our character was facing
- how they thought of GPT
- the feature: what GPT feature they used
- how it solved their problem

I learnt this from my Instagram carousel days

- everything is going fine
- then there's a problem
- it gets bigger
- but there's hope
- present the solution

My ideas weren't fully along this, but something similar.

### That and Silence.

The first batch happens and we get the WhatsApp message that they really loved the idea.

But we never really heard back on what happened of these.
Later, we did 2 more batches of such ideas in the coming months.

And wrote some problem statements for the Codex hackathon that was coming.

### A Big Surprise

Around May, it had been roughly 5 months that I hadn't been home. So I took WFH for a week and went home.

By that time, I had almost forgotten that we had done anything like this.

One evening, I was watching IPL - as we do in summers. And an Ad aired -
I saw a kid trying to fix his dad's old bike. He pulled out his phone and spoke into GPT, and it hit me.

This was one of my ideas!

Not just that - after a break I saw another one!!

I was very excited to see something that I made - make it to the TV.
It was very unexpected to be honest.

I quickly pull out my phone. And texted the leader.
"Hey I just saw the ideas that we wrote for GPT - they made it into a TV ad!"

They too were unaware that this had happen. I quickly searched YouTube to find it and shared with them. Meanwhile, they texted the contact at OpenAI to verify.

This was true- it was our work that made it to the TV ad.

I shared the news with our team as well.

Everyone was very happy on this little milestone!

I watched the add with little bit of whimsy each time it showed up on TV.
I still remember the tune that played in the background lol!

These were officially posted on ChatGPT India's Instagram handle, YouTube and the ads I mentioned.
