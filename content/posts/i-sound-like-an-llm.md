It's 2027, you arrive on my blog - take a glimpse, read a few lines and you think - yup this is AI writing. Close the tab and move on with your time.

## Present day

I am a software engineer (whatever the underlying title might say - Applied AI, FDE or any other).

As soon as I reach office, I run `brew update && brew upgrade`. I go fill up my water bottle, come back, and run `claude`.

> "/obsidian-sync what does the day look like today?"

Claude brings up a page worth of content - in my terminal.

> "You have to address 3 bugs, finish up the xyz feature you were finishing. And make these 5 little changes in the background."

Hmm... I take a read of the agent's output. I might have already forgotten the 5 little findings from yesterday.

I take a sip. The reading begins.

That's the biggest part of my job. Reading.

## I read more than I ever have

I proofread AI's text, try to shape its thinking, form ideas. Write more text in the form of instructions, read, make sure it has the right details.

Afternoon. I may have already done 3M tokens on Claude easily.
Of which:

- 30-40% might be simple commands - like ls, sed, edit, grep and more.
- 30-40% would be its thought trace. Or the post-thought decisions, to be scientifically correct.
- 10-20% is the final reading - plans, beginnings, conclusions and in-between stats and reporting back.

Decision making - reading the LLM's thought trace, correcting its plans.

Well, most of the fatigue - comes from processing tokens. I am probably processing as much information as Mr Claude Opus. Okay, maybe not as much. At least, my inference is probably more optimal than it.

All of my work - is reading text.

I don't read a lot outside of work.
People say it easily that you should read more - books, fiction.
I haven't been able to read more. At least, read more of what I want or am recommended.

## Yes, I write like an AI

So, why am I writing this post?

This is less of a rant. More of a signal, "what you read is how you write" ~ me.

I do spend my entire day reading AI output. And if my lil output starts sounding more like AI...

Hmm, "if my lil output" - see? I didn't even say "writing", XD.

Anyways, happy reading. AI made us into readers.
